The work ["Concise and Logically Consistent Conformal Sets for Neuro-Symbolic Concept-Based Models"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18202) with Samuele Bortolotti, Emanuele Marconato, Andrea Passerini and Stefano Teso has been accepted at NeurIPS 2026!

This paper studies how to quantify uncertainty in neuro-symbolic concept-based models while respecting the logical constraints linking concepts and labels.
We identify three key requirements for conformal prediction in this setting: consistency, coverage and conciseness.
We then introduce COCOCO, a post-hoc framework that constructs prediction sets for concepts and labels jointly and reconciles them through logical reasoning, retaining distribution-free coverage guarantees.
We finally showcase the effectiveness of our approach across eight datasets, obtaining competitive performance and compact prediction sets.
